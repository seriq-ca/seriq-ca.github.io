A meeting of the SEMTL community will be held on **Thursday, Feb 20th, 2025**, at **09:30 to 12:30**. It will take place in the **ER Building (2155 Guy St), room 1072, Concordia University**. Coffee and pastries will be available during the meeting.

### Registration

Please RSVP using [this form](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1fG5uRQbvPufsGLUAnelnuzTSneUVe6L1RDAD7ZTWeIE/edit?gid=1297457195#gid=1297457195).
