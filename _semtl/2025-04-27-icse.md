Last week, many SEMTL participants attended the [47th International Conference on Software Engineering](https://conf.researchr.org/home/icse-2025), April 27th to May 3rd, in Ottawa, Canada.

<img src="https://semtl.github.io/img/2025-05-05/icse.jpg" alt="semtl_at_icse" style="width:100%;"/><br>
