{%- comment -%} The home title's wire network. Inline SVG rather than a stack of gradients: a graph needs branches and turns, and the two colours have to follow the theme, which a background-image cannot do. Purely decorative, so it is hidden from assistive tech. Styled in _sass/_decorations.scss. Geometry, in viewBox units, which are CSS pixels at full width: the bus runs at y=26 between two risers that turn up at x=3 and x=517, bracketing the title above. Four branches tap the bus downward and end in nodes — everything above the bus frames, everything below it branches. Every segment is orthogonal; every node is 6 units centred on a 2-unit run, so centres land on whole units. {%- endcomment -%}
