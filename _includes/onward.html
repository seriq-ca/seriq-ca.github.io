{%- comment -%} Where the home page hands off to the rest of the site. The destinations are resolved by `ref` in the page's own language, the same lookup the header nav uses, so a page that does not exist in a language drops out of the list rather than linking across languages. Labels come from `home.onward` in _data/i18n/*.yml — longer than the nav's, since these stand alone. Emits nothing when none of the three pages exist.
